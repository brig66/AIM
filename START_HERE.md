## 5-Minute Quick Start

### 1. Upload Files (2 minutes)
Upload to `/wp-content/plugins/aim-ai-visibility/`:
- aim-ai-visibility-plugin.php
- templates/admin-page.php
- assets/admin-style.css
- assets/admin-script.js

### 2. Activate (1 minute)
- Go to WordPress Plugins menu
- Find "AIM AI Visibility Technical Suite"
- Click "Activate"

### 3. Configure (2 minutes)
- Go to Dashboard → AI Visibility
- Fill in your company info
- Click "Save Settings"

**Done!** Schema is now being generated on your site.

## Common Questions

### Q: Will this change my website?
**A:** No. The plugin adds invisible structured data that AI engines read, but visitors see no changes.

### Q: How long does it take to set up?
**A:** 5 minutes to install, 15-20 minutes to configure, depends on you.

### Q: What if I make a mistake?
**A:** Click "Undo Last Changes" button in the admin to revert to any previous version.

### Q: Can I deactivate it later?
**A:** Yes. Go to Plugins menu and click "Deactivate". Schema stops being generated immediately.

### Q: Do I need to update it?
**A:** No. This is version 1.0.0 and is self-contained. No external dependencies.

### Q: Does it cost anything?
**A:** No. This is included with your AIM assessment.

### Q: What if something breaks?
**A:** The plugin is read-only on the frontend. It only affects the admin dashboard. No risk to your site.
